About T

A practical perspective on your next home.

Before real estate, I spent years working around construction and residential properties. That experience taught me to ask questions, pay attention to details and understand how a home fits the people who live in it. Today, I bring that perspective and local knowledge to buying and selling in Monmouth County. I use current market research and modern tools to keep you informed, and explain your options in plain language.

Home Ground

Get to know the towns.

The Shore isn’t one kind of place. Start with these five towns, then compare the commute, surroundings and day-to-day details that matter to you. Open a town below for a closer look.

Atlantic HighlandsView details +

Harbor & ferry access

A harbor town with ferry service to New York City. When comparing homes, consider the trip to the terminal as well as your everyday errands.

If commuting is part of your plan, we can start with your schedule and compare locations from there.

HighlandsView details +

Waterfront & outdoor recreation

A waterfront borough with scenic views and outdoor recreation. Look beyond the listing photos and consider how the location works for your daily routine.

We can compare access, parking and the questions to raise about a particular property before making an offer.

Local information for Highlands ↗
Red BankView details +

Downtown & riverfront

Red Bank combines a downtown of shops and restaurants with public spaces along the Navesink River. Distance to downtown can change how you use the town.

We can compare the actual route from a home to the places you expect to visit, rather than relying on a map pin.

Local information for Red Bank ↗
Sea BrightView details +

Beach access

The municipal beach is a central part of Sea Bright. If beach access is your priority, compare the route from each home and where you would park.

Think through how you plan to use the home throughout the year, and which property-specific questions need answers.

RumsonView details +

Space & daily routes

Start your Rumson search with how much space you need, how you use the yard and where you travel each day. Those details help narrow a broad property search.

We can compare the home, grounds and everyday routes together, and identify the details worth following up on.
